⚛️ Line Spectra Lab

An interactive electron energy & light challenge
18 questions • 3 levels

Mission: Decode the light!

Electrons absorb energy to jump upward and release light when they fall to lower energy levels. Discover how the size of a jump determines the light produced.

How to play: Complete all 6 questions in each level. Select an answer and press Check Answer. If you're incorrect, read the hint and try again. Correct answers unlock the next question. Your first-attempt accuracy is tracked separately.
